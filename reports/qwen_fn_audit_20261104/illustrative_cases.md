# Illustrative cases: partial exploratory assessment

These 11 selected examples are not representative and are not independently adjudicated label errors. A is an apparent explicit conflict, B is ambiguous. The article and criteria quotations are checked exact substrings of the extracted fields. The complete abstract and criteria are in the CSV.

## Muthu_2021 / source row 5818 / B

- ID: muthu_2021|openalex_id=https://openalex.org/w2021865356
- Title: The Efficacy of Systematic Active Conservative Treatment for Patients With Severe Sciatica
- Article evidence: "symptoms and clinical findings that would normally qualify them for surgery"
- Criteria evidence: "The study must be related to spine surgery involving preoperative or intraoperative or postoperative variables."
- Assessment: Both described arms are conservative treatment; whether this satisfies the broad spine-surgery relevance requirement needs protocol/stage clarification. Surgery eligibility alone is not surgery.

## van_Dis_2020 / source row 14532 / B

- ID: van_dis_2020|pmid=https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10815116|doi=https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.283.19.2529|openalex_id=https://openalex.org/w2019284559
- Title: Cognitive-behavioral therapy, imipramine, or their combination for panic disorder: A randomized controlled trial.
- Article evidence: "cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT) only (n=77)"
- Criteria evidence: "used CBT combined with medication or pill placebo"
- Assessment: Combination arms are present, but the trial also contains a CBT-only arm; whether the review excluded the whole study or only combination comparisons is unclear.
